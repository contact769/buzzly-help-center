# Analytics & Statistics

Buzzly shows you clear charts so you can understand how your newsletter and website are doing — no spreadsheets required.

## Your dashboard at a glance

When you log in, the main **Dashboard** greets you with the headline numbers:

- Total **subscribers**
- Number of **published posts**
- Average **open rate** (how many people open your emails)
- **Engagement charts** showing trends over time

---

## The four things you can measure

| Area | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Audience** | Total subscribers, how fast you're growing, and where new readers came from. |
| **Engagement** | How many people **open** and **click** your emails, plus subscribes vs. unsubscribes. |
| **Post performance** | How each individual issue did, your **top posts**, and the **best hours** when readers open. |
| **Web traffic** | Visits to your [website](./website-builder.md), your most popular pages, and where visitors came from. |

---

## Choosing a time range

Every chart lets you pick a period so you can zoom in or out:

- **7 days**
- **30 days**
- **90 days**
- **1 year**
- **All time**

---

## A note on open rates

Open tracking works through a tiny invisible image in each email. Some email apps block these images or load them automatically, so **open rates are a helpful trend, not a perfectly exact count**. Watch the direction over time rather than obsessing over a single number.
