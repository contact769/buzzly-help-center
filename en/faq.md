### Getting started

**How do I create my newsletter?**
Register, verify your email with the 6-digit code, and you're in. Full walkthrough: **[Getting Started](./getting-started.md)**.

**Can I change my newsletter address (prefix) later?**
No — your prefix (`yourname.mybuzzly.com`) is permanent once you sign up. Pick it carefully. See **[Getting Started](./getting-started.md#step-1--create-your-account)**.

**Is Buzzly available in my language?**
Buzzly works in **English and French**. Switch anytime with the language button in the header.

### Writing & sending

**Can I preview a post before sending it?**
Yes. [Step 2 of the editor](./creating-posts.md#step-2--preview-see-it-before-you-send) shows the exact email, and you can send a **test email** to yourself first.

**Can I schedule a post for later?**
Yes, in [Step 3 of the editor](./creating-posts.md#step-3--distribute-send-or-publish) — pick a future date and time. Scheduling is a **Pro** feature; see **[plans](./settings.md#your-buzzly-plan)**.

**Can I send the same email design every week?**
Save it as an **[Email Template](./email-templates.md)** and start new posts from it.

**Can a post go to email AND my website?**
Yes — choose email, website, or both in [Step 3](./creating-posts.md#step-3--distribute-send-or-publish).

### Subscribers

**How do I import my existing readers?**
Upload a CSV with `email`, `firstName`, `lastName`. See **[Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md#import-a-whole-list-csv)**. (CSV import is a **Pro** feature.)

**What happens when someone unsubscribes?**
They stop getting emails but remain in your list marked **Unsubscribed**, and can resubscribe later. See **[Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md#what-happens-when-someone-unsubscribes)**.

### Money

**How do I make money from my newsletter?**
Offer **paid subscriptions**. You'll need the **Premium** plan and a verified **Stripe** account. Full guide: **[Monetization](./monetization.md)**.

**Who handles the payments?**
**Stripe** does, securely. Money goes straight to your bank account — Buzzly never holds your funds. See **[Monetization](./monetization.md#getting-paid)**.

**What's the difference between my Buzzly plan and reader subscriptions?**
Your **Buzzly plan** is what you pay to use Buzzly. **Reader subscriptions** are what your readers pay you. See the [side-by-side comparison](./monetization.md#two-kinds-of-subscription--dont-mix-them-up).

### Website & growth

**Do I need to know how to code to build my website?**
No. You pick a theme and customize it visually. See **[Your Website](./website-builder.md)**.

**Can I edit my website without it going live?**
Yes — your site stays private until you click **Publish**. See **[Your Website](./website-builder.md#saving-and-publishing)**.

**How can I grow faster?**
Turn on **[Referrals](./referrals.md)** so readers earn rewards for bringing friends.

---

### Results

**Are the open-rate numbers exact?**
They're a reliable **trend**, not a perfect count, because some email apps block the tracking image. See **[Analytics](./analytics.md#a-note-on-open-rates)**.

**Does Buzzly work offline?**
No — it needs an internet connection to load and send.
