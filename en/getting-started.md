# Getting Started

⏱️ **About 10 minutes** · This page takes you from zero to a live newsletter.

← Back to the **[Help Center home](./README.md)**

---

## Step 1 — Create your account

1. Go to the Buzzly sign-up page and click **Register**.
2. Fill in your **email**, a **password**, and your **first and last name**.
3. Choose your **newsletter prefix** — this is the short name that becomes your web address, for example `yourname` in `yourname.mybuzzly.com`.
   - As you type, Buzzly checks in real time whether the name is available. A green check means it's yours.
   - ⚠️ **Pick carefully — your prefix cannot be changed after sign-up.**
4. Click **Register**.

### Verify your email

After registering, Buzzly emails you a **6-digit code**.

1. Open the email and copy the code.
2. Enter it on the verification screen.
3. That's it — you're taken straight to your **Dashboard**.

> **Didn't get the code?** Check your spam folder, wait a minute, then use the "resend" option on the verification screen.

---

## Step 2 — Log in (any time after)

1. Go to the login page.
2. Enter your **email** and **password**.
3. You land on your Dashboard.

**Forgot your password?** Click **Forgot password** on the login screen, enter your email, and follow the reset link we send you.

> **Security note:** If you log in from a new device or location, Buzzly may ask you to confirm your identity to keep your account safe. Just follow the on-screen prompt.

---

## Step 3 — Set up your newsletter

Before your first send, take a couple of minutes in **[Settings](./settings.md)** to:

- Add your **newsletter name and logo**.
- Set your **sender name and reply-to email** so readers know who's writing.
- Write a **welcome email** (optional) that new subscribers receive automatically.

You can always come back and change these later.

---

## Step 4 — Write your first post

Head to **[Creating & Publishing Posts](./creating-posts.md)** and follow the 3-step editor. You'll write your content, preview exactly how it looks in an inbox, and choose how to send it.

---

## Step 5 — Add your first readers

Open **[Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md)** to add readers one by one or import a whole list from a spreadsheet.
