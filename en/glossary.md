# Glossary

Plain-English definitions of the words you'll see around Buzzly.

← Back to the **[Help Center home](./README.md)**

---

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Post** | A single newsletter issue — what you write and send. See **[Creating Posts](./creating-posts.md)**. |
| **Draft** | A post you've saved but haven't sent or published yet. |
| **Scheduled post** | A post set to send automatically at a future date and time. |
| **Block** | A building piece of a post (heading, paragraph, image, button, etc.). |
| **Email template** | A saved post design you can reuse. See **[Email Templates](./email-templates.md)**. |
| **Subscriber** | A person who receives your newsletter. Also called a **reader**. See **[Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md)**. |
| **Distribution / Diffusion** | Buzzly's internal name for your subscriber list. |
| **Theme (tag)** | A label to organize posts and group subscribers. See **[Themes & Tags](./themes-and-tags.md)**. |
| **Prefix** | The unique short name in your web address, e.g. `yourname` in `yourname.mybuzzly.com`. Chosen at sign-up and permanent. |
| **Website builder** | The visual, no-code tool for building your public site. See **[Your Website](./website-builder.md)**. |
| **Section** | A reusable block on your website (hero, articles, signup form, FAQ, etc.). |
| **Link in Bio / Simple Blog / News Site** | The three website styles you can choose from. |
| **Open rate** | The share of subscribers who opened a given email. See **[Analytics](./analytics.md)**. |
| **Click rate** | The share of subscribers who clicked a link in your email. |
| **Monetization** | Earning money from your newsletter via paid subscriptions. See **[Monetization](./monetization.md)**. |
| **Tier** | A paid plan you offer your readers (e.g. "Supporter — €5/month"). |
| **Stripe** | The trusted payment company that processes reader payments and pays you. |
| **Stripe Connect** | The setup that links your bank account so you get paid directly. |
| **Premium post** | A post only paying members can read in full. |
| **Referral program** | A system that rewards readers for bringing friends. See **[Referrals](./referrals.md)**. |
| **Milestone** | A referral goal (e.g. 5 friends) that unlocks a reward. |
| **Integration** | A connection between Buzzly and another tool, like Discord. See **[Integrations](./integrations.md)**. |
| **Buzzly plan** | Your own subscription to Buzzly (Starter / Pro / Premium). See **[Settings](./settings.md#your-buzzly-plan)**. |
| **Welcome email** | An automatic message new subscribers get when they join. |
| **CSV** | A simple spreadsheet file (`.csv`) used to import subscribers in bulk. |
| **Prefix / subdomain** | Your newsletter's own web address on Buzzly. |
