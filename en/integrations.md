# Integrations

**Integrations** connect Buzzly to other tools you already use, so they work together automatically.

## Where to find them

Go to **Integrations** in your dashboard sidebar. You'll see the available connections and whether each one is active.

---

## Discord

If your community lives on **Discord**, you can connect it to Buzzly. Once linked, Buzzly can coordinate with your Discord server — for example, mapping roles so your members and subscribers stay in sync.

**To connect Discord:**

1. Open **Integrations**.
2. Choose **Discord** and follow the prompts to authorize the connection.
3. Configure any options (such as role mapping) offered on the screen.

---

## Connect a form or store to Buzzly

Typeform, Formstack, Elementor, OptinMonster, Gumroad, WooCommerce, ManyChat, Kajabi, ClickFunnels, Memberful, Patreon and Thinkific all connect the same way: Buzzly gives you a unique URL, and you paste it into that tool's webhook settings. Every time it fires, Buzzly adds (or updates) a subscriber — no approval process, no third-party account linking.

**To set up a webhook:**

1. Open **Integrations**.
2. Find the platform, and click **Connect**.
3. Optionally give it a label (e.g. "Homepage contact form") and choose whether new subscribers get your welcome email.
4. Click **Generate URL** and copy it immediately — for security it is shown only once. If you lose it, open the integration's **Configure** panel and click **Regenerate**.
5. Paste the URL into the platform, following that platform's own guide:
