# ClickFunnels

Turn ClickFunnels contacts into newsletter subscribers.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. In [ClickFunnels (2.0)](https://www.clickfunnels.com), go to Workspace Settings → Webhooks → Add New Endpoint.
3. Paste the URL and select the `contact.created` event.
4. Save, then submit a test contact through your funnel.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing arrives.** Confirm you selected `contact.created` specifically — ClickFunnels lists several similarly named events.
