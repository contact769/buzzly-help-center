# Gumroad

Turn Gumroad purchases into newsletter subscribers.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. In [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com), go to Settings → Advanced.
3. Paste the URL as your Ping endpoint.
4. Save, then make a test purchase (or use Gumroad's test mode) to confirm.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing arrives.** Confirm the Ping endpoint field was actually saved — Gumroad silently ignores an invalid URL rather than showing an error.
