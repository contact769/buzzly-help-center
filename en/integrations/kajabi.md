# Kajabi

Turn every Kajabi purchase and form submission into a newsletter subscriber.

## Prerequisites

- Requires a Kajabi Growth or Pro plan — webhooks aren't available on lower tiers.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. In [Kajabi](https://kajabi.com), go to Settings → Webhooks (or a specific Offer's checkout settings).
3. Add the URL as a webhook endpoint.
4. Save, then send a test purchase or form submission.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing arrives.** Confirm your Kajabi plan includes webhooks — this is the most common cause.
