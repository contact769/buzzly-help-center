# ManyChat

Turn ManyChat conversation data into newsletter subscribers.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. In [ManyChat](https://manychat.com), name your email field `email` and your name fields `first_name`/`last_name`.
3. Add the URL as an External Request (webhook) action in your automation.
4. Save, then test by triggering the flow.

## Troubleshooting

**The field-naming rule.** The field name itself must be in English, even if the visible label is in another language.

**No name on subscribers.** Double-check your fields are named `email`/`first_name`/`last_name` — translating the technical field name is the most common cause of missing names.
