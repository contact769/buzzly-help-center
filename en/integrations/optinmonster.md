# OptinMonster

Turn OptinMonster campaign opt-ins into newsletter subscribers.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. In [OptinMonster](https://optinmonster.com), name your email field `email` and your name fields `first_name`/`last_name`.
3. Add the URL as a webhook/external-request integration on the campaign.
4. Save, then test with a real opt-in.

## Troubleshooting

**The field-naming rule.** The field name itself must be in English, even if the label shown to visitors is in another language.

**No name on subscribers.** Double-check your fields are named `email`/`first_name`/`last_name` — translating the technical field name is the most common cause of missing names.
