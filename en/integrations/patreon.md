# Patreon

Turn Patreon memberships into newsletter subscribers.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com) requires you to create your own client in its developer portal.
3. Add the URL as a webhook endpoint for members events on that client.
4. Save, then trigger a test member event.

## Troubleshooting

**A patron doesn't show up after a real pledge.** Patreon has occasionally been reported to send an empty email address on some member events — if a patron doesn't show up as a subscriber, that's the likely cause, not a broken webhook.
