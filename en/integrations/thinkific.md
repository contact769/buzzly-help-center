# Thinkific

Turn Thinkific signups into newsletter subscribers.

## Prerequisites

- Requires the Thinkific Grow plan.

## Steps

1. In Buzzly, generate your webhook URL above and copy it.
2. In your [Thinkific](https://www.thinkific.com) webhook settings, add a new webhook.
3. Use the `user.signup` event.
4. Paste the URL and save, then create a test signup.

## Troubleshooting

**Nothing arrives.** Confirm your Thinkific plan includes webhooks (Grow or above) and that you selected the `user.signup` event.
