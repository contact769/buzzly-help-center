# Monetization: Paid Subscriptions

Buzzly lets you **earn money from your newsletter** by charging readers for premium content. This page explains how paid subscriptions work and how to set them up.

← Back to the **[Help Center home](./README.md)**

**Related:** [Creating Posts](./creating-posts.md) · [Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md) · [Your Settings](./settings.md) · [FAQ](./faq.md)

---

## How it works, in plain English

1. You offer one or more **paid plans** (called **tiers**) — for example "Supporter — €5/month."
2. Readers pay to join a tier through a secure checkout.
3. Some of your **[posts](./creating-posts.md)** can be marked **premium**, so only paying members get the full issue.
4. The money goes to **your own bank account**, handled securely by **Stripe** (a trusted global payment company). Buzzly never touches your funds directly.

---

## What you need before you start

To turn on paid subscriptions, two things must be true:

1. **You're on the Premium plan.** Monetization is a Premium feature. Check or upgrade in your **[Subscription settings](./settings.md#your-buzzly-plan)**.
2. **You've completed Stripe verification.** Because real money is involved, Stripe needs to confirm your identity and bank details (a legal requirement called "Know Your Customer"). It's a one-time guided setup — you'll fill in some personal and banking information, and Stripe handles the rest.

> This connection uses **Stripe Connect**, which means you get paid directly and Buzzly simply passes buyers through to your secure Stripe checkout.

---

## Setting it up

1. Go to **Monetization** in your dashboard sidebar.
2. If you haven't connected Stripe yet, follow the prompt to **verify your identity and link your bank account**. Complete every step Stripe asks for — payouts won't start until it's finished.
3. **Create your paid tier(s):** give each a name, a price, and how often readers are billed (e.g. monthly).
4. Save. Your paid options now appear to readers at checkout and on your site.

---

## Getting paid

- Readers subscribe and pay through **Stripe's secure checkout** — you don't handle any card details yourself.
- **Payouts go straight to your linked bank account** on Stripe's schedule.
- Your readers can **manage their own subscription** (update payment, cancel) through a self-service portal, so you don't have to handle billing questions manually.

---

## Marking a post as premium

When you write an issue, you can choose to make it **premium** so only paying members receive the full content. Free readers can be shown a preview that invites them to upgrade. You set this while creating the post — see **[Creating & Publishing Posts](./creating-posts.md)**.

---

## Tracking your revenue

The **Monetization** page gives you a **revenue overview** so you can see how your paid subscriptions are performing. For audience and engagement numbers, see **[Analytics & Statistics](./analytics.md)**.

---

## Two kinds of "subscription" — don't mix them up

This trips people up, so here's the difference:

| Term | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Your Buzzly plan** | What *you* pay Buzzly to use the platform (Starter / Pro / Premium). Managed in [Settings → Subscription](./settings.md#your-buzzly-plan). |
| **Reader subscriptions** | What *your readers* pay *you* for premium content. Managed here in Monetization. |
