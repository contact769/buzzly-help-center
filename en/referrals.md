# Referrals

A **referral program** rewards your existing readers for bringing their friends. It's one of the most effective (and free) ways to grow a newsletter — your happiest readers become your best marketers.

← Back to the **[Help Center home](./README.md)**

---

## How it works

1. Each subscriber gets a **unique referral link** to share.
2. When friends subscribe through that link, the referrals are counted.
3. When a reader hits a **milestone** (e.g. 3 referrals, 10 referrals), they **unlock a reward** you've defined.

It turns growth into a fun, rewarding game for your audience.

---

## Setting it up

1. Go to **Referrals** in your dashboard sidebar.
2. **Turn on** the referral program.
3. Define your **milestones and rewards** — for example, "Refer 5 friends → get an exclusive guide."
4. Save.

Your readers can then start sharing and earning.

---

## Fulfilling rewards

When a reader earns a reward, you'll see it so you can **deliver what you promised** (send the guide, apply the perk, etc.). Keeping rewards genuinely valuable is what keeps the program working.

---

## Keeping it fair

Buzzly watches for **suspicious activity** (like fake or self-referrals) so your program rewards real growth, not gaming. If something looks off, it gets flagged for review.
