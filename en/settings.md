# Your Settings

Settings is where you control the details of your newsletter, your emails, your website, and your Buzzly plan. Find it under **Settings** in your dashboard sidebar.

← Back to the **[Help Center home](./README.md)**

---

## The settings sections

| Section | What you set here |
|---|---|
| **General** | Your name, company, and basic account details. |
| **Newsletter** | Your brand name, logo, sending frequency, and default author. |
| **Email** | Your default subject, **reply-to** address, and **welcome email**. |
| **Website** | Design options for your [public site](./website-builder.md). |
| **Subscription** | Your **Buzzly plan** and upgrades. |

> **Saving:** A **Save** button appears only when you've made changes. If you don't see it, everything is already saved.

---

## Newsletter branding

Set the **name** and **logo** that appear on your emails and public pages. This is what makes your newsletter recognizable, so it's worth getting right early. You did some of this in **[Getting Started](./getting-started.md)** — refine it here anytime.

---

## Email settings

- **Reply-to address:** where replies from readers land. Use an inbox you actually check.
- **Welcome email:** an optional message new subscribers receive automatically the moment they join. A warm welcome sets the tone — it's highly recommended.

---

## Your Buzzly plan

Buzzly offers different plans (typically **Starter**, **Pro**, and **Premium**). Higher plans unlock more features:

- **Scheduling posts** for later → Pro and up.
- **Importing subscribers** from a CSV → Pro and up.
- **[Paid subscriptions / monetization](./monetization.md)** → Premium.

Go to **Settings → Subscription** to see your current plan and upgrade. This is *your* subscription to Buzzly — separate from any **[paid subscriptions your readers buy from you](./monetization.md#two-kinds-of-subscription--dont-mix-them-up)**.

---

## One thing you can't change

Your **newsletter prefix** (the `yourname` in `yourname.mybuzzly.com`) is **locked after sign-up**. Choose it carefully during **[registration](./getting-started.md#step-1--create-your-account)**.
