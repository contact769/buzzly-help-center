# Themes & Tags

**Themes** (also called **tags**) are labels you create to organize your content and your readers. Think of them like folders or categories.

← Back to the **[Help Center home](./README.md)**

**Related:** [Creating Posts](./creating-posts.md) · [Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md)

---

## What themes do

A theme has a **name** and an optional **description**. You can attach a theme to two things:

1. **Posts** — Tag your issues by topic (e.g. "Product updates," "Weekly roundup," "Interviews"). This keeps your archive tidy and helps readers find what interests them.
2. **Subscribers** — Group your readers (e.g. "Beginners," "Pros," "Paying members") so you can keep track of different audiences.

---

## Creating a theme

1. Go to **Themes** in your dashboard sidebar.
2. Click **New Theme**.
3. Give it a **name** and, if you like, a short **description**.
4. Save.

You can create as many as you need, and edit or remove them anytime.

---

## Using themes

- **On a post:** In [Step 1 of the post editor](./creating-posts.md#step-1--compose-write-your-content), pick one or more themes for the issue.
- **On a subscriber:** Open a reader's profile from **[Managing Subscribers](./managing-subscribers.md)** and assign a theme to group them.

---

**Tip:** Keep your list of themes short and meaningful. A handful of clear categories is far more useful than dozens of overlapping ones.
